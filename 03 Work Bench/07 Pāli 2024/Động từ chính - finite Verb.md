****Các loại** verb (**động từ** trong tiếng Anh)**

- Auxiliary Verbs (Trợ **động từ**)

A finite verb is ==a verb that shows agreement with a subject and is marked for tense, number, and person==: 

- **Agreement with a subject**: Finite verbs agree with the subject in a sentence. 
    
- **Tense**: Finite verbs can be in the present tense or past tense. 
    
- **Number and person**: Finite verbs distinguish person and number. 
    

Finite verbs can be the only verb in a sentence or the central element of the main clause. They can also be imperative verbs. For example, "James does the washing every day" is a finite verb in the present tense, "Even Dave did the washing yesterday" is a finite verb in the past tense, and "Do the washing, Josie!" is a finite verb in the imperative mood. 

Finite verbs are different from non-finite verbs, which do not show tense, person, or number. Non-finite verbs include infinitives, participles, and gerunds.
