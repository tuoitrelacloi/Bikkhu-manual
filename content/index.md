> [!NOTE] Ayaṁ tathāgatassa pacchimā vācā.
> Atha kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi: “handa dāni, bhikkhave, āmantayāmi vo: ‘vayadhammā saṅkhārā, appamādena sampādethā’”ti.
