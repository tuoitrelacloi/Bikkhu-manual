Below is the **standard Theravāda / Aṭṭhakathā-based Pāli terminology** used to explain the **25 ways of committing adinnādāna** (taking what is not given).  
Minor **wording variants exist across commentaries**, but these terms are the ones most commonly taught in Vinaya manuals and ethical handbooks.

---

## **25 Pāli Terms for Adinnādāna (Theft)**

### **A. Taking by One’s Own Action (10)**

1. **Sāhatthika-adinnādāna** – taking by one’s own hand
    
2. **Theyyacitta-adinnādāna** – stealing with intent
    
3. **Mussāvādena adinnādāna** – taking by lying
    
4. **Kuhakavatthena adinnādāna** – taking by fraud or trickery
    
5. **Tulākūṭa-kaṃsakūṭa-mānakūṭena** – cheating in weights, measures, or counting
    
6. **Paṭicchanna-adinnādāna** – secret theft
    
7. **Paccaya-paṭirūpakena** – taking by false claim of entitlement
    
8. **Sāhasikena** – taking by force or violence
    
9. **Upanidhāya gaṇhāti** – substitution or switching goods
    
10. **Anāpucchā-paribhoga** – using without permission
    

---

### **B. Causing Another to Steal (5)**

11. **Āṇattiyā adinnādāna** – ordering another to steal
    
12. **Pāyogena adinnādāna** – instigating theft
    
13. **Nissaggiyena adinnādāna** – arranging conditions for theft
    
14. **Sampahaṃsanena adinnādāna** – approving or encouraging theft
    
15. **Lābhena adinnādāna** – knowingly benefiting from stolen goods
    

---

### **C. Abuse of Law, Authority, or Position (5)**

16. **Vinicchaya-kuṭa** – corrupt judgment
    
17. **Sakkhi-kuṭa** – false witness
    
18. **Rāja-kuṭa** – abuse of government or authority
    
19. **Dhamma-kuṭa** – misuse of law or rule
    
20. **Adhikāra-kuṭa** – abuse of entrusted power
    

---

### **D. Deceptive Retention & Misuse (5)**

21. **Visaṃvādanena laddha-paribhoga** – keeping goods gained by deception
    
22. **Bhāga-atireka-gahaṇa** – keeping excess given by mistake
    
23. **Vetana-apadāna** – withholding wages or due payment
    
24. **Nikkhipita-bhaṇḍa-vināsa** – misuse of entrusted property
    
25. **Saṅghika-paribhoga-micchācāra** – misusing communal or donated property
    

---

## **Key Vinaya Definition**

> **“Adinnaṃ theyyacittena ādiyati.”**  
> _Taking what is not given, with the intention to steal._

This list is **analytical, not permissive**—used to sharpen **sīla (moral restraint)** and **paññā (discernment)**, especially regarding subtle forms of dishonesty.
