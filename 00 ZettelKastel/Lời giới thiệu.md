| What now needs to be explained is the method by which the minds of those seeking ordination are trained so as to become suitable members of the Sangha community.                                                                                                                                                                         | Now we need to explain how people who want to become monks are trained so that they are ready to live as members of the Sangha community. |
| It should be done by the  introduction of the Buddha Dhamma  into their minds and by embedding the  Dhamma in their minds. There is no  other way to condition the minds. It can  never be done by the enforcement of  rules or other methods of control.  There is no other way to produce good  bhikkhūs than conditioning their minds. | Good bhikkhus are formed only by teaching and firmly establishing the Buddha’s Dhamma in their minds, not by forcing rules or control.    |
